About FinanceAviator

Useful tools without the clutter.

FinanceAviator builds small, focused calculators for the financial decisions people run into most — buying a home, paying down debt, planning for retirement, and everything in between.

Our approach

Clear inputs. Honest numbers.

Every calculator on this site is built around the same idea: enter your numbers, see the result, and understand exactly how we got there. No account required, no email gate, no dark patterns.

We keep the math transparent and the design out of the way, so the answer is the thing you notice — not the interface.

10 calculators live

Why we built this

Good financial tools shouldn't be hard to find.

A lot of financial calculators online are buried behind ads, missing the details you actually need, or built for one narrow case. FinanceAviator started as a simple collection of the calculators we kept wishing existed — and it's grown from there.

We're a small, independent project. If there's a calculator you think is missing, let us know.
